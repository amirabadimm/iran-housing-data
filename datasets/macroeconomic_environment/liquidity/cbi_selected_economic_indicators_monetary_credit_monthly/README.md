# CBI Selected Economic Indicators: Monthly Monetary and Credit Data

## Overview

Monthly Central Bank of Iran reports covering banking-system balance sheets, major monetary and credit indicators, and related tables. The standardized output extracts liquidity, money, and quasi-money.

## Provider

- Organization: Central Bank of the Islamic Republic of Iran
- Publishing unit: Economic Statistics Department
- Official page: https://cbi.ir/category/2692.aspx
- Acquisition method: manual download

## Coverage

The registered collection contains 240 monthly reports with complete Solar Hijri coverage from 1385-01 through 1404-12:

- 180 PDF reports from 1385-01 through 1399-12
- 60 Excel reports from 1400-01 through 1404-12

Original filenames, canonical relative paths, acquisition timestamps, byte counts, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `metadata/file_registry.csv`.

## Storage and lineage

Raw reports remain byte-preserved under:

`data/raw/macroeconomic_environment/liquidity/cbi_selected_economic_indicators_monetary_credit_monthly/<year>/`

The standardized output is:

`data/standardized/macroeconomic_environment/liquidity/cbi_selected_economic_indicators_monetary_credit_monthly/monthly_liquidity.csv`

It is generated by:

`pipelines/standardization/build_cbi_monthly_liquidity.py`

## Extraction and validation

- PDF reports are parsed from their embedded text using coordinate-aware extraction.
- Excel reports are parsed using the provider's native worksheet and row labels.
- Historical Persian and Arabic numeral variants are normalized.
- Values are reported in thousand billion rials.
- Every row must satisfy `liquidity = money + quasi-money` within the documented tolerance.
- The output must contain exactly one row for each of the 240 expected months.

Native Persian labels remain only inside extraction code where they are required to match the source documents. Maintained documentation, metadata, paths, and analytical field names are English.
